Reading data from technical drawings

For quotations, master data and production planning, information from drawings is copied by hand again and again: drawing number, material, tolerances, revision. Reading it can be automated, even for customer drawings with an unfamiliar title block.

How it works

  1. Collect drawings

    From a folder, a PDM export or as an attachment to a customer enquiry. Vector PDFs and scans are handled the same way.

  2. Read the title block and tables

    The fields of the title block, the revision table and parts lists are recognised, even if the layout differs from customer to customer.

  3. Find information in the view

    Fits, surface specifications and notes such as heat treatment are located and output as a list, each with an excerpt.

  4. Compare with the previous revision

    If an older revision exists, the comparison lists the changed information, for example as a note to production planning.

  5. Transfer to your systems

    As a file for master data, costing or inspection planning. The drawing stays linked.

Typical use
Requests for quotation, master data maintenance, engineering change management
Formats
PDF (vector or scan), TIFF
Title blocks
ISO 7200 and customer-specific layouts
Your effort afterwards
Spot checks and flagged cases

What is automated

  • Reading the title block, revision table and parts list
  • List of fits and surface specifications with their location
  • Comparison of two revisions at text level

What is not automated

  • No geometry evaluation and no conversion into CAD models
  • Dimension chains and tolerance analyses remain with your design department
  • Unclear information is flagged, not guessed
