From the first call to ongoing operation

Before you sign a contract, you see figures from your own company. After that we carry the risk: acceptance criteria before the start, a 3-month trial period with right of return.

  1. Intro call, 30 minutes

    You tell us which documents or questions cost your team the most time and where a mistake would be most expensive. We tell you whether this can be automated. Free of charge, by video. Your IT is welcome from the start.

  2. Check, 5 working days

    You upload up to 20 sample documents. We run a trial extraction and deliver an Excel file, the error rate per field, a savings calculation and a one-page decision memo for management.

  3. Contract and acceptance criteria

    Before the start we agree in writing what the worker has to achieve, for example 95% correctly read values per field or 18 of 20 test questions answered correctly. The check is credited in full towards the rate.

  4. Setup, 2 to 4 weeks

    There is a first version after 2 weeks; after that we review weekly with your contact person until the criteria are met. Setup is included in the rate.

  5. Trial period, 3 months

    The worker runs in day-to-day business. If it does not convince you, you return it at the end of a month without giving reasons.

  6. Operation, 24 months

    We monitor quality, add new formats and documents and install updates. After that, cancellable monthly.

Time you should plan for
  • Check: about 1 hour to select and upload the documents
  • Setup: one contact person for about 1 hour per week
  • Operation: review flagged cases only

Data protection and security

Your documents contain supplier data, design know-how and sometimes personal data. This is how we handle them:

  • Text recognition and language models run on our own servers in the EU. We only use external AI services if you approve this in writing beforehand.
  • Data processing agreement from the first order, with a list of all sub-processors
  • encrypted transfer and encrypted storage
  • Access only for the people looking after your contract
  • Your documents are not used to train models
  • Export of your data at any time; deletion 30 days after the end of the contract or after a check without a contract

Who does the work

A large part of the work is automated: text recognition, extraction, plausibility checks, reports. That makes a fixed monthly rate possible.

Before anything reaches you, a person looks at it. Every setup is checked manually before acceptance; for the Knowledge Worker, the answers to the agreed test questions.

Everything we set up is documented. With the handover documentation your IT or another provider can take over operation at any time.
