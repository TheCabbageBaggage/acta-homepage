From the first call to ongoing operation

After each step you decide again whether to continue. Before you spend money on a pilot, you see figures from your own company.

  1. Intro call, 30 minutes

    You tell us which documents or questions cost your team the most time. We tell you whether this can be automated and suggest a package. Free of charge, by video.

  2. Check, 5 working days

    You upload up to 20 sample documents. We run a trial extraction and deliver an Excel file, the error rate per field and a savings calculation with your volumes. We discuss the result in a half-hour meeting.

  3. Pilot, 6 weeks

    Before the start we agree in writing what the pilot has to achieve, for example 95% correctly read values per field. There is a first version after 2 weeks, and after that we review progress weekly with your contact person.

  4. Acceptance

    Together we check against a test set whether the criteria are met. Only then is the second half of the pilot price due. If they are not met, we make improvements.

  5. Operation

    The solution runs on a server in the EU. We monitor recognition quality, add new formats and install updates. Can be cancelled monthly after the minimum term, annually for the knowledge assistant.

Time you should plan for
  • Check: about 1 hour to select and upload the documents
  • Pilot: one contact person for about 1 hour per week
  • Operation: review flagged cases only

Data protection and security

Your documents contain supplier data, design know-how and sometimes personal data. This is how we handle them:

  • Text recognition and language models run on our own servers in the EU. We only use external AI services if you approve this in writing beforehand.
  • Data processing agreement from the first order, with a list of all sub-processors
  • encrypted transfer and encrypted storage
  • Access only for the people working on the project
  • Your documents are not used to train models
  • Deletion 30 days after the end of the project unless there is an operation contract

Who does the work

A large part of the delivery is automated: text recognition, extraction, plausibility checks, reports. This keeps prices low.

Before anything reaches you, a person looks at it. Every report and every pipeline is checked manually before handover; for the knowledge assistant, the answers to the agreed test questions.
