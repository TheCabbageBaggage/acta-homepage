Frequently asked questions

Data and security

Where are our documents processed?

On our own servers in the EU, which also run text recognition and the language models. We only use external AI services such as OpenAI or Anthropic if you approve this in writing beforehand. When you order, we conclude a data processing agreement. Your documents are not used to train models and are deleted 30 days after the end of the contract.

Who has access to our data?

Only the people looking after your contract, and for server operation the hosting provider under the data processing agreement. Transfer and storage are encrypted, and every access is logged.

What does our IT get?

A technical data sheet with hosting provider, data centre location, encryption, access concept, deletion periods and the list of sub-processors. If you like, your IT joins the intro call.

What happens if acta.systems drops out?

Your results and the configuration belong to you and can be exported in open formats at any time. With the handover documentation your IT or another provider can take over operation. If we cannot deliver the agreed service, your obligation to pay ends.

Process, acceptance and trial period

What do you need from us to start?

For the document check, up to 20 sample documents, the list of fields you need, and your monthly volume. For the Knowledge Worker, the approved documents and 20 questions your employees often ask. You upload them after ordering in the online shop.

What happens if the acceptance criteria are not met?

The criteria are fixed before the start, for example the accuracy per field. If we do not meet them, we make improvements. If that does not work, you return during the trial period.

How does the trial period work?

The first 3 months after acceptance are a trial period. During this time you can return at the end of any month without giving reasons. You only pay for the months up to then.

How much of our time does setup take?

One contact person for about one hour per week for the reviews. In addition, selecting the sample documents at the beginning.

Do we have to sign a contract after the check?

No. The check stands on its own and has its own result. If you sign within 60 days, it is credited in full.

Prices and payment

How do we order and pay?

You order the document check directly in the online shop. For the Document Worker and Knowledge Worker you receive an offer and a contract after the check. The rate is paid monthly by invoice or direct debit.

Why a monthly rate instead of a project price?

Because you get an ongoing service, not a one-off project: operation, monitoring, updates and adjustments are part of it. The rate comes from your operating budget, so an investment approval is usually not needed.

Is there a setup fee?

No. Setup is included in the rate, and the check is credited in full if you sign within 60 days.

What does it cost if we need more than agreed?

We quote additional work in writing beforehand, at €130 per hour or €950 per day net.

Technology

Can you connect to our ERP?

The Document Worker delivers Excel, CSV or an import file in the format your ERP can read. We program a direct interface on request at a fixed price.

Does this work with handwritten entries?

Printed and digital documents are the normal case. Handwriting is read, but always submitted for review. The document check shows whether that is sufficient for your documents.

Can technical drawings be evaluated as well?

Yes: title block, revision table, parts list, fits and surface specifications. We do not evaluate geometry. See the example.

Do we have to install anything?

No. The Document Worker and Knowledge Worker run on our servers in the EU. You use them in the browser or receive the results as a file.
