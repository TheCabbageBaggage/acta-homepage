Frequently asked questions

Your question is not listed? Write to us.

Data and security

Where are our documents processed?

On our own servers in the EU, which also run text recognition and the language models. We only use external AI services such as OpenAI or Anthropic if you approve this in writing beforehand. When you order, we conclude a data processing agreement. Your documents are not used to train models and are deleted 30 days after the end of the project unless there is an operation contract.

Who has access to our data?

acta.systems is a sole proprietorship: the founder has access, and for server operation also the hosting provider under the data processing agreement. Transfer and storage are encrypted.

Process and acceptance

What do you need from us to start?

For the document check, up to 20 sample documents, the list of fields you need, and your monthly volume. For the knowledge assistant, the approved documents and 20 questions your employees often ask. You upload them after ordering in the online shop.

What happens if the pilot does not reach its targets?

The acceptance criteria are fixed before the start, for example the accuracy per field. If we do not meet them, we make improvements. The second instalment is only due on acceptance.

How much of our time does a pilot take?

One contact person for about one hour per week for the reviews. In addition, selecting the sample documents at the beginning.

Do we have to order a pilot after the check?

No. The check is a separate package with its own result.

Prices and payment

How do we order and pay?

Directly in the online shop. Payment is handled by Stripe, and the invoice is sent automatically. Pilot packages are paid in two instalments: on order and after acceptance.

Is there a setup fee for operation?

No. For the knowledge assistant, half of the pilot price is also credited towards the first year of operation if you sign up within 30 days.

What does it cost if we need more than the package includes?

We quote additional work in writing beforehand, at €130 per hour or €950 per day net.

Technology

Can you connect to our ERP?

In the pilot we deliver Excel, CSV or an import file in the format your ERP can read. We program a direct interface on request at a fixed price.

Does this work with handwritten entries?

Printed and digital documents are the normal case. Handwriting is read, but always submitted for review. The document check shows whether that is sufficient for your documents.

Can technical drawings be evaluated as well?

Yes: title block, revision table, parts list, fits and surface specifications. We do not evaluate geometry. See the example.

Do we have to install anything?

No. The pipeline and the knowledge assistant run on our servers in the EU. You use them in the browser or receive the results as a file. For the server security check you run a check script on your server once; for monitoring, a small monitoring service is set up there.
