Finding deadlines and reminding in time

Periodic inspections, calibrations, maintenance contracts, supplier certificates: the dates are in certificates and contracts, but rarely in one place. We read them and remind the responsible person before anything expires.

How it works

  1. Read in existing documents

    Certificates, reports and contracts from a folder or archive. Once for the existing documents, then continuously for new ones.

  2. Find dates and rules

    Date of the last inspection, interval, expiry date or notice period. If the document only states the interval, the next date is calculated and marked as calculated.

  3. Assign responsibility

    By asset, cost centre or document type. You define the rules together with us during setup.

  4. Remind in time

    For example 60 and 30 days in advance, with a reference to the original document. If there is no response, a second message goes to the deputy.

  5. File the new evidence

    When the new certificate is uploaded, the next deadline starts automatically.

What is automated

  • Reading dates, intervals and notice periods from documents
  • Calculating and marking the next due date
  • Reminder with source to the responsible person

What is not automated

  • You or your specialist decide which inspection obligations apply to your equipment
  • We do not replace authorised inspectors or legal advice
  • If a document is missing, no deadline can be found. The deadline list shows such gaps
