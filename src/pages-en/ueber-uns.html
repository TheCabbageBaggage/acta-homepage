About acta.systems

acta.systems grew out of hands-on work in industrial companies: maintenance, spare parts management, production planning. There we saw how many hours a month go into typing values from paper and searching for documents.

Why we exist

A mid-sized industrial company that wants to use AI today usually hires an agency or builds something itself with off-the-shelf tools. An agency project is often too large for a first attempt. If you build it yourself, operation remains with your own IT.

We take a third route: you rent a ready AI worker at a fixed monthly rate. Setup and operation are on us. It starts with a trial extraction of your own documents, and a contract only follows if the figures support it.

What we bring

  • many years of experience in maintenance, spare parts management and production planning
  • software development and operation of servers in the EU
  • our own pipelines for text recognition, extraction and knowledge search, which we keep improving

How we work

  • Verifiable results. Acceptance criteria are fixed before the start, and you can check every result yourself.
  • Limits stated openly. If a case is not worthwhile, we say so in the intro call or at the latest in the check.
  • A source for every value. Every extracted value and every answer refers to the place in the document.
  • No lock-in. Results and configuration belong to you. With the handover documentation your IT or another provider can take over at any time.

What we do not do

  • no work for private individuals
  • no IT security audits, penetration testing or forensics
  • no blocks of hours without an agreed result
