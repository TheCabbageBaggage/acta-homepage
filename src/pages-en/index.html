What you can hold us to

Four commitments that apply to every package.

Every answer gives its source

Values and answers refer to the document and page, for drawings to the title block or view. Uncertain values are flagged and only transferred after confirmation.

Your data stays in the EU

Processing on servers in the EU under a data processing agreement. We delete project data 30 days after the end of the project unless operation has been agreed.

Fixed price and fixed delivery time

You know the price and the date before you order. Anything outside the scope we quote in writing beforehand.

Acceptance against criteria

We agree the target accuracy per field or the share of correctly answered test questions with you before the start. The second instalment is only due after acceptance.

Background in maintenance and production

Behind acta.systems are many years in maintenance, spare parts management and production planning, plus software development and Linux operations. We know from our own working life how an inspection report is structured and who types the values into the ERP today.
